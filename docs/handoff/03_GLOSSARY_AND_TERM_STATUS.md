# Glossary and Term Status

## Reading key

- **Established**: standard mathematical or computational term.
- **Adapted**: standard term with Warhammer-specific use.
- **Custom**: proposed label from this research program.
- **Hypothesis**: testable claim rather than settled definition.

## Abstract List Geometry

**Status:** Custom.

A representation of an army list as a structured relational object before board placement. It includes unit capabilities, rules, resource access, mission disposition, interaction operators, and context-dependent coverage.

A raw radar chart is not sufficient because pair relations and rule couplings carry essential information.

## Action Funnel

**Status:** Custom.

A progressively narrowed set of legal actions which remain competitive under a selected objective, risk model, and opponent model.

Possible formal versions:

- actions within \(\epsilon\) of optimal expected value
- actions maximizing a robust lower bound
- non-dominated actions on a Pareto frontier

## Board-State Geometry

**Status:** Custom or adapted.

The geometric, topological, graph, and combinatorial structure of one concrete board state. It includes positions, movement regions, visibility, terrain, objective control, engagement constraints, and unit status.

## Bundle-Like Family

**Status:** Adapted.

A context-indexed collection of spaces resembling fibers over a base, without a proven local-triviality property.

## Category of Elements

**Status:** Established.

Given a functor or indexed family \(H:B\to\mathbf{Set}\), the category of elements assembles all pairs \((b,h)\) with \(h\in H(b)\). This gives a precise alternative to informal fiber language.

## Constraint Envelope

**Status:** Adapted.

A boundary generated by a family of constraints, legal states, or reachable outcomes. Depending on construction, the envelope may be a curve, surface, polytope, support boundary, or Pareto frontier.

## Constraint Space

**Status:** Adapted.

A space whose coordinates and inequalities encode legal or effective limits. Examples include movement reach, target eligibility, save thresholds, resource budgets, and scoring conditions.

## Coupling Operator

**Status:** Adapted.

A rule which links stages or factors which otherwise separate. Examples include a critical hit which bypasses a wound roll or a wound effect which changes save resolution.

## Dice Event

**Status:** Established probability term, adapted use.

A subset of the finite dice sample space. A 4+ event on one D6 is \(\{4,5,6\}\).

## Dice Space

**Status:** Adapted.

The finite sample space of all relevant dice outcomes, with an associated probability measure and rule-defined transformations.

## Differential Geometry

**Status:** Established.

Study of smooth manifolds, tangent spaces, curvature, differential forms, and smooth maps. Warhammer needs differential geometry only after a smooth representation or local relaxation is defined.

## Discrete Geometry

**Status:** Established.

Study of geometric structures built from finite or countable objects. Warhammer rule states, grids, thresholds, and finite outcome sets fit naturally here.

## Fiber

**Status:** Established.

For a projection \(\pi:E\to B\), the fiber over \(b\) is \(\pi^{-1}(b)\).

In the proposed game model, a fiber may contain legal histories or reachable states associated with one fixed pre-game context.

## Fiber Bundle

**Status:** Established.

A quadruple \((E,B,\pi,F)\) with local product structure \(\pi^{-1}(U)\cong U\times F\). Warhammer should not be called a true fiber bundle without proving local triviality on a restricted domain.

## Force-Disposition Geometry

**Status:** Custom.

The mission and scoring structure induced by the pairing of Force Dispositions, including which actions, board regions, or unit roles generate victory points.

## Game-Space Projection

**Status:** Custom or adapted.

A map from a richer state or history space into a lower-dimensional observable such as damage, objective control, or victory points.

## Geometric Hole

**Status:** Custom.

A context region where a list lacks adequate capability, reachability, reliability, or scoring response.

A geometric hole requires a defined context space, metric or adjacency relation, and capability threshold. Without these, the phrase remains informal.

## Generative Geometry

**Status:** Adapted.

A rule system which produces larger geometric structure from a compact coordinate or parameter set.

## Hybrid System

**Status:** Established.

A dynamical system combining continuous variables and discrete modes. Warhammer combines continuous positions with discrete phases, rules, wounds, resources, and dice events.

## Identifiability

**Status:** Established.

A parameter is identifiable when distinct parameter values produce distinguishable observations under a model. Local and global forms exist.

## Indexed Family of Spaces

**Status:** Established.

A mapping which assigns one space \(H_b\) to each context \(b\). This is the safest formal starting point for the proposed list-to-board construction.

## Inner Product

**Status:** Established.

A bilinear positive-definite form which defines lengths and angles in a vector space. Orthogonality requires an inner product or an equivalent structure.

## Inverse Generative Scaffold

**Status:** Custom.

A hidden structural model reconstructed from sparse measurements, after which a generative process produces a larger geometry or observable surface.

## Inverse Problem

**Status:** Established.

Recover hidden causes, parameters, or structure from observed effects or partial measurements.

## Isotropy

**Status:** Established.

Directional invariance under a chosen group of transformations. Equal numerical axis values do not establish isotropy without compatible units, normalization, and metric.

## List-to-Board Fiber Evolution

**Status:** Custom.

An intuitive label for a context-indexed family of board trajectories generated from fixed list and mission parameters. Formal replacements include indexed family, stratified fibration, or dependent state space.

## Manifold

**Status:** Established.

A space locally resembling Euclidean space. A finite rules system with thresholds is not automatically a manifold. Some relaxed or fixed-combinatorial regions may admit manifold structure.

## Measure Transformation

**Status:** Established.

A change to probability assignment on a sample space. Rerolls and replacement mechanics can often be represented through transformed measures or transition kernels.

## Metric

**Status:** Established.

A function \(d(x,y)\) defining distance and satisfying non-negativity, identity, symmetry, and triangle inequality.

Any claim about closeness, strain length, or geometric holes needs a metric or a weaker explicit relation.

## Operator Graph

**Status:** Custom or adapted.

A directed graph whose nodes represent values or intermediate states and whose edges represent rule operators. It supports compilation, dependency analysis, coupling detection, and counterfactual tracing.

## Orthogonality

**Status:** Established.

Two vectors are orthogonal when their inner product is zero. In modeling, "orthogonal" should be reserved for proven geometric orthogonality or replaced by separable, independent, uncoupled, or distinct-factor.

## Outcome Shadow

**Status:** Custom.

A low-dimensional observable produced by projection from a richer game state or history. Final VP score is an outcome shadow.

## Pair Strain

**Status:** Custom, with standard log-ratio mathematics.

For positive paired values \(a,b\):

\[
\sigma(a,b)=\log(a/b)
\]

Balance lies at zero. Positive and negative signs encode advantage direction. Magnitude encodes multiplicative separation.

## Parameter Space

**Status:** Established.

A space whose points select models, contexts, or system configurations. Army lists, missions, terrain, and rules versions can form components of a parameter space.

## Piecewise-Defined Map

**Status:** Established.

A function with different formulas or outputs on different regions. The Strength-Toughness wound rule is piecewise-defined.

## Policy

**Status:** Established in control, games, and reinforcement learning.

A rule for choosing actions from states. Player skill enters a computational model through policy class and policy quality.

## Product Space

**Status:** Established.

A space formed from combinations of component spaces. List, mission, terrain, and deployment contexts naturally form a product or dependent product.

## Projection Map

**Status:** Established.

A map from a richer space to a reduced representation. Projection may forget information, aggregate states, or compute an observable.

## Projection Stack

**Status:** Custom.

A sequence of maps from raw rules and values through relations, thresholds, dice events, state transitions, board histories, and VP outcomes.

## Quantizer

**Status:** Established in signal processing and applied mathematics.

A map from a continuous or finely discrete input into a smaller set of output bins. The wound table quantizes Strength-Toughness relation into required D6 thresholds.

## Reachability

**Status:** Established in control theory and formal methods.

Study of states or outcomes accessible from an initial state under legal actions and disturbances.

## Relational Typing

**Status:** Custom.

Assign a value role based on how a value interacts with another value under a specific operator and context.

## Ruler-Like Value

**Status:** Custom metaphor.

A value acting as extent, magnitude, capacity, or scale in a relation.

## Scoring Envelope

**Status:** Custom.

A boundary or support representation for reachable or probable VP outcomes from a state.

## Separability

**Status:** Established.

A model is separable when its function, measure, or optimization can factor into simpler components. A clean hit-wound-save sequence may factorize. Special rules often break separability.

## Sparse Identifiable Coordinates

**Status:** Custom phrase built from established concepts.

A compact coordinate set which supports reconstruction while remaining distinguishable under the model.

## State Abstraction

**Status:** Established.

A reduced state representation preserving selected predictions or control properties. Bisimulation and homomorphism provide formal versions.

## Stochastic Game

**Status:** Established.

A multi-agent dynamical system with probabilistic transitions and strategic action choice.

## Stratified Space

**Status:** Established.

A space decomposed into pieces, or strata, each with regular structure. Threshold rules and changing legal-action graphs make stratified models plausible for Warhammer.

## String-Art Envelope

**Status:** Informal label with established envelope mathematics.

A curve appearing as the envelope of a parameterized family of lines connecting points on axes. Equal axis scales do not generally produce a circle.

## Sufficient Coordinate Set

**Status:** Custom or adapted.

A coordinate subset preserving a selected family of outputs within defined error bounds. It differs from a sufficient statistic unless a probabilistic inference framework is specified.

## Threshold Surface

**Status:** Adapted.

A boundary separating rule regimes. In a Strength-Toughness plane, lines such as \(S=T\), \(S=2T\), and \(2S=T\) partition wound-threshold regions.

## Topology

**Status:** Established.

Study of continuity, connectivity, neighborhoods, and invariants preserved under continuous deformation. Topology can model reachability or connected action regions after a topology is explicitly defined.

## Value Function

**Status:** Established.

Expected or robust future utility from a state under policy assumptions. A VP value function formalizes projected scoring advantage.

## Value Space

**Status:** Custom or adapted.

A finite or continuous space formed from all legal values of one characteristic or a tuple of characteristics.

## VP Space

**Status:** Custom or adapted.

The two-dimensional space of score pairs \((VP_A,VP_B)\).

## VP Strain

**Status:** Custom.

A score-advantage coordinate, usually \(VP_A-VP_B\), or a future value difference under policy assumptions.

## Wound Quantizer

**Status:** Custom label for a standard piecewise map.

A map from Strength-Toughness relation into required wound roll.
