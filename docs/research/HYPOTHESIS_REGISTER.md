# Active Hypothesis Register

The complete hypothesis set lives in `docs/handoff/06_FALSIFIABLE_HYPOTHESES_AND_TESTS.md`.

This register selects Build Week claims.

## BW-H1: Exact pair projection

**Claim:** A selected scalar pair can map through a documented relation, threshold partition, finite D6 subset, and effect distribution without empirical game data.

**Test:** Exhaustively enumerate every D6 outcome and compare against the projection engine.

**Pass:** Exact equality.

**Fail:** Any unexplained mismatch.

## BW-H2: Structural holes exist beyond aggregate ratings

**Claim:** Two lists with similar aggregate ratings can differ materially in relational coverage.

**Test:** Construct fixtures with equal or near-equal aggregate scores and one seeded uncovered target region.

**Pass:** Coverage analysis finds the seeded region while aggregate rating does not isolate it.

**Fail:** Coverage adds no actionable distinction.

## BW-H3: Minimal counter-profile closes a known hole

**Claim:** A targeted profile substitution can close an absolute hole with less total change than broad stat inflation.

**Test:** Compare candidate replacements under fixed list-size or resource constraints.

**Pass:** At least one candidate closes the region with lower intervention cost.

**Fail:** Detector cannot distinguish targeted repair from arbitrary power increase.

## BW-H4: Explanations can remain grounded

**Claim:** GPT-5.6 can explain computed findings without unsupported numerical claims when supplied a constrained evidence object.

**Test:** Evaluate explanations against claim-to-evidence links.

**Pass:** Every number and causal statement resolves to deterministic output.

**Fail:** Any unsupported number or invented rule appears.

## BW-H5: Generic proxy values preserve demonstration coherence

**Claim:** A proxy dataset based on independently entered scalar facts can produce coherent relational structure without copied names, prose, or presentation.

**Test:** Review fixture provenance, labels, distributions, and visual presentation.

**Pass:** Demo remains coherent and contains no protected identifiers or copied expression.

**Fail:** Understanding depends on recognition of the source game or copied presentation.
